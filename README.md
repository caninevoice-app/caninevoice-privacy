# CanineVoice Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

CanineVoice is a dog behavior observation and interaction logging application. We respect user privacy and are committed to minimizing the collection of personal information.

## Information We Collect

CanineVoice does not require users to create an account or log in.

The current version of CanineVoice does not collect personal data through our servers.

Dog profiles, observation records, context information, and interaction history created in the app are stored locally on the user's device.

## Camera and Microphone

CanineVoice may request access to the device camera and microphone when the user chooses to record a dog observation.

Camera and microphone access is optional and only activated after the user grants permission.

These permissions are used to support user-initiated dog behavior observations. CanineVoice does not continuously record the camera or microphone in the background.

## Data Sharing

CanineVoice does not sell personal information.

The current version does not transmit user observation records to CanineVoice servers and does not share those records with third parties for advertising or tracking.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored CanineVoice data remains on the user's device until the user deletes it, resets the app data, or removes the application, subject to the device operating system's storage and backup behavior.

Users can delete locally stored observation history through the app's available deletion or reset functions.

## Scientific and Medical Disclaimer

CanineVoice is designed to assist users in organizing observations of dog behavior and interactions.

CanineVoice does not claim to translate dog language, read a dog's thoughts, or provide veterinary diagnosis.

When available information is insufficient, the app may indicate that a reliable determination cannot be made.

Information provided by CanineVoice is not a substitute for professional veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Children's Privacy

CanineVoice is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through CanineVoice-operated servers in the current version.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CanineVoice features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised update date.
